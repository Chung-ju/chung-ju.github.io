abstract: >-
  We present MinT (MindLab Toolkit), a managed infrastructure system for Low-Rank Adaptation (LoRA) post-training and online serving, designed for scenarios where many trained policies are derived from a small set of costly base-model deployments. Rather than saving each policy as a fully merged checkpoint, MinT keeps the base model in memory and routes LoRA adapter revisions through stages such as rollout, update, export, evaluation, serving, and rollback. MinT scales along three axes. Scale Up: it extends LoRA RL to frontier dense and Mixture-of-Experts architectures (including MLA and DSA attention paths), validated beyond 1T total parameters. Scale Down: by moving only exported LoRA adapters (potentially under 1% of base-model size in rank-1 settings), MinT achieves an 18.3x step reduction on a 4B dense model and 2.85x on a 30B MoE; concurrent multi-policy GRPO further reduces wall time by 1.77x and 1.45x respectively. Scale Out: MinT separates durable policy addressability from CPU/GPU working sets, supporting 10^6-scale addressable catalogs, while packed MoE LoRA tensors improve live engine loading by 8.5-8.7x.
